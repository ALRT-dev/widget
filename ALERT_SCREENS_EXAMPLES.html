THE ONLY CARD THAT WRITES ITS LEVEL
“Watch and Act” is the national phrase, not ALRT’s — so it is written verbatim in the header. Saturated brand gradient, prescribed triangle reproduced not restyled, and every directive kept verbatim with “NSW RFS advises:” in front. ALRT’s own inferences are hedged (“may reduce”).
COMMUNITY IS FULLY SEPARATE

THE ONLY SHAPE WITH ALRT'S OWN VOICE
White fill, single-weight outline, level carried by the border colour — matching the outline diamond treatment. Always labelled “ALRT Assessment”, always citing the named sources it is built from. The level is set by the Intel process, never by AI.
THE FOUR SHIELD LEVELS
Black outline — Advisory. Situational awareness; no action suggested. Yellow #FFC400 — Elevated. Could affect people in the area; stay across updates. Orange #FF6B00 — Significant. Credible developing risk; consider avoiding the area. Red #E4002B — Critical. Serious risk to people; act on the advisory's guidance.
NEVER RESTATES OR OVERRIDES AN OFFICIAL WARNING
If an agency has issued a warning, that alert appears in its own shape and takes precedence — the shield only adds ALRT's assessment around it. Timestamp says “last checked”, not “updated”, and the primary button points at the official source.
