THE ONLY CARD THAT WRITES ITS LEVEL
“Watch and Act” is the national phrase, not ALRT’s — so it is written verbatim in the header. Saturated brand gradient, prescribed triangle reproduced not restyled, and every directive kept verbatim with “NSW RFS advises:” in front. ALRT’s own inferences are hedged (“may reduce”).
COMMUNITY IS FULLY SEPARATE
No community content on this card, at all. A report of smoke near this fire renders as its own tinted circle in the feed and on the map — near this card because it is near it in the world, never inside it, never upgrading it.
FOLLOWING = THE UPDATE STREAM
Shown in its Following state: each new RFS update becomes a push notification and a new dot on the Updates timeline.
