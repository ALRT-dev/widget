Style and flow notes

  • Blended colour throughout: the purple band carries a warm orange glow in one corner and a magenta bloom in the other, the CTA is a magenta to violet gradient with a soft top highlight, the welcome screen leads with the ALRT mark on a warm-lit tile, seat segments and avatars use the same gradients. No flat blocks anywhere.
  • No dashes: every line rewritten with commas and full stops. Middot separators stay, matching your member rows.
  • Opt-in live woven in: the gate sheet sells the snapshot first and mentions live as a per-trip switch, with the control rule in the lavender box. Same consent language family as your scheduled check-ins screen.
  • Success screen has one job: celebrate briefly, show the 7 open seats, and push the single highest value action, inviting family. The trial reminder promise sits in the lavender box so trust is built at the moment of purchase.
  • Billing banner is calm, not scary: amber card in the blended style, states the grace period plainly, one action. Family screen stays visible but dimmed underneath so the stakes are shown, not shouted. Driven by the RevenueCat billing issue webhook.
